## Every IT person has a Raspberry Pi at home

> “Every proper IT person has at least one Raspberry Pi or Arduino at home — and nothing to do with it.”

Yet you can do “so much silly stuff” with it — Mirek has a Raspberry Pi 3 and a Raspberry Pi Pico lying on his desk. A good topic for a chat.

## Push-to-talk in Google Meet

When your microphone is muted and the browser window has focus, **hold the spacebar** — the microphone is on for as long as you hold it. Like a walkie-talkie. The instructor mutes everyone during the lecture, so it is handy for questions.

## When the instructor talks fast, stop him

Mirek admits that once he gets going he speaks fast. Feel free to interrupt — “blame it on a friend who messaged you that he is going too fast”. His goal: pass on as much as possible, and rather stop and consolidate than pour in new things that no longer fit. “Squeeze as much out of me as you can.”

## Dates according to XKCD

For the different ways of writing dates (European day.month, American month/day), XKCD has a comic about a localization working group: the US and EU teams agree to meet on the “same” date — and the Europeans turn up a month later. The punchline: use ISO 8601.
